1. Introduction

UrbanSynth X is a 32-voice hybrid polyphonic synthesizer engineered by Urban Audio Music for macOS (VST3 / AU) and iPadOS / iOS (Standalone & AUv3).

  • Dual-Layer Hybrid Synth Engine: INST 1 (Cyan) and INST 2 (Magenta) featuring PolyBLEP oscillators, Sample Playback Engine, 4-Pole Stereo VCF, and a real-time 3D Analyzer.
  • 3-Track 64-Step Live Sequencer: 8 chainable banks (A–H), per-step modulation, algorithmic pattern generator, and direct DRAG MIDI / DRAG WAV export.
  • Urban Drum 8: 8 channels, 73 controls, 400 sounds (DRM1, Rytm, DFAM, Syntakt), and the V / E / M / S instant rhythm matrix with 32 hardware-inspired sequences.

2. Installation Notes

macOS (VST3 & Audio Unit)

  • Run the signed and notarized installer UrbanSynth_X_V3.5_Installer.pkg.
  • Installed paths: ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/UrbanSynth X.vst3 and ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/UrbanSynth X.component.
  • Restart your DAW (Ableton Live, Logic Pro, Cubase, etc.) and rescan plugins.

iPadOS & iOS (Standalone & AUv3)

Install UrbanSynth X on iPad or iPhone: ready to play immediately in Standalone mode (USB/Bluetooth MIDI, Ableton Link) or loaded as an AUv3 instrument inside your mobile DAW.

3. Navigation

  • Top Bar: Preset Browser, BPM, MIDI Map, Undo/Redo, Panic, XY-Pads, 64-Seq, and Settings.
  • Main Synth Panel: Sample Playback, Digital Oscillators, 4-Pole Filter & LFO, ADSR Envelopes, Dual FX, and 3D Analyzer.
  • 64-Step Live Sequencer (64-SEQ): full-screen sequencer view for TRK 1, TRK 2, and TRK 3.
  • Urban Drum 8 (DRUM LAB): complete 8-channel percussion console.
  • Quick Bar & Keyboard: theme selector (STYLE: CYBER / URBAN), XL KEYS, pitch/mod sliders, and playable keyboard.

4. Using the Top Bar

  • XY-PADS / 64-SEQ: instant access to the dual-axis performance pad and the 64-step sequencer.
  • MIDI MAP / CLEAR CC: quickly assign any parameter to your hardware MIDI controller.
  • BPM & PANIC: synced tempo readout and instant all-notes-off voice reset.
  • Preset Browser (< / >): browse factory banks and user presets with full recall of synth layers, Drum 8, and sequences.
  • Settings (Gear Icon): access Advanced, Configuration, MIDI, Share, Manual, and About panels.

5. Understanding Sound & Synthesis

The signal path blends Sample Playback (with drag & drop WAV/AIFF/MP3 and SOLO SAMPLE) with Digital Oscillators (PolyBLEP) featuring SYNC 2>1, routed into a 4-Pole Stereo VCF with Drive, Key-Tracking, Analog Slop, and dedicated LFO. The 3D Analyzer displays Sample Shaper, VCF curve, ADSR envelope, and a live 32-voice oscilloscope in real time.

6. Features & Controls

Arpeggiator & Performance

Independent clock (ARP BPM, TAP, DIVIDE), ARP ON, OCT, REP, UNISON, DETUNE, HOLD, 16V MONO, and direct DRAG MIDI / DRAG WAV export.

64-Step Live Sequencer & V / E / M / S Matrix

3 independent tracks (TRK 1 Cyan, TRK 2 Magenta, TRK 3 Urban Drum 8) across 8 banks (A–H). The gold DRUM LAB bar opens the drum machine; the 4 buttons V DRM1 / E RYTM / M DFAM / S SYNT load 32 complete rhythm sequences matched to their respective kits.

Urban Drum 8 (400 Sounds)

8 channels (KICK, DRUM 1, DRUM 2, MULTI, SNARE, HI-HAT 1, HI-HAT 2, CLAP) with 50 sounds each: 01–13 DRM1, 14–26 RYTM, 27–38 DFAM, 39–50 SYNT. Includes HH CHOKE, INSERT DRIVE, TRIG MODE, and Pioneer Beat Roll (1/8, 1/16, 1/32, 1/64).
